# Leave of Absence Policy

**Policy ID:** HR-003  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Human Resources Department  
**Last Reviewed:** September 2025

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs the types of leave available to Acme Corp employees, the eligibility requirements, the approval process, and the impact of leave on employment status and benefits. Acme Corp is committed to supporting employees through personal and medical circumstances while ensuring operational continuity.

---

## 2. Types of Leave

### 2.1 Medical Leave

Employees who are unable to perform their duties due to a serious health condition — their own or that of an immediate family member — may apply for medical leave. Medical leave is job-protected for up to **12 weeks per calendar year** in accordance with applicable employment laws (e.g., FMLA in the US).

**Eligibility:** Minimum 6 months of continuous employment.

**Required documentation:**
- Written request submitted at least **30 days in advance** for foreseeable leave, or as soon as practicable for emergency leave.
- Medical certification from a licensed healthcare provider (HR-supplied form, to be returned within 15 days of request).
- Completed Leave of Absence Request form in the HR Portal.

**Pay during medical leave:** Employees may use accrued PTO concurrently with medical leave. Short-term disability insurance (if enrolled) kicks in after a 7-day waiting period.

### 2.2 Personal Leave

Employees may request unpaid personal leave for reasons not covered by other leave types (e.g., family relocation, personal development, caregiving). Personal leave is not job-protected by law but Acme Corp will make reasonable efforts to hold the position for up to **4 weeks**.

**Eligibility:** Minimum 1 year of continuous employment, no active performance improvement plans.

**Required fields for personal leave requests:**
- Employee name, department, and employee ID
- Reason for leave (general description; detailed personal information is not required)
- Requested start and end date
- Coverage plan: who will handle critical responsibilities during absence

### 2.3 Parental Leave

Acme Corp provides **16 weeks of fully paid parental leave** for primary caregivers and **4 weeks of fully paid leave** for secondary caregivers, available within the first year of a child's birth, adoption, or foster placement.

Parental leave requests must be submitted via the HR Portal at least **8 weeks before** the anticipated start date.

### 2.4 Bereavement Leave

Employees may take up to **5 paid days** for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling) and up to **3 paid days** for an extended family member. No prior approval is required; notify your manager and HR as soon as possible.

---

## 3. Approval Process

All leave requests (except bereavement) follow this approval chain:

| Leave Type      | Step 1                  | Step 2              | Step 3 (if > 4 weeks) |
|-----------------|-------------------------|---------------------|-----------------------|
| Medical         | HR Coordinator          | HR Business Partner | VP of Human Resources  |
| Personal        | Direct Manager          | HR Business Partner | N/A                   |
| Parental        | HR Coordinator          | VP of Human Resources | N/A                 |

Decisions are communicated within **5 business days** of receiving all required documentation.

---

## 4. Benefits During Leave

- **Health insurance:** Continues during approved leave. Employee must continue to pay their share of premiums.
- **PTO accrual:** Pauses during unpaid leave; resumes upon return.
- **Retirement contributions:** Employee contributions pause during unpaid leave; employer matching resumes upon return.
- **Equity vesting:** Follows individual grant terms; consult HR for specifics.

---

## 5. Return to Work

Employees returning from medical leave must provide a **fitness-for-duty clearance** from their healthcare provider before resuming work. HR will coordinate a return-to-work meeting within the first week back.

---

## 6. Contact

For leave inquiries, contact your **HR Business Partner** or email **leave@acmecorp.com**.
