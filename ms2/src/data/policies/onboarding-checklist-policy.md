# New Hire Onboarding Checklist Policy

**Policy ID:** HR-002  
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Human Resources Department  
**Last Reviewed:** August 2025

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the standard onboarding process for all new employees joining Acme Corp. It outlines the tasks, timelines, and responsible parties required to ensure a consistent, welcoming, and productive start for every new hire.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to HR Business Partners, hiring managers, IT administrators, and new employees across all departments and locations.

---

## 3. Pre-Arrival Checklist (HR & Manager — 1 Week Before Start Date)

| Task | Owner | Deadline |
|------|-------|----------|
| Send offer letter and contract | HR | 2 weeks before start |
| Collect signed documents (NDA, tax forms, emergency contact) | HR | 1 week before start |
| Set up employee profile in HRIS | HR | 3 days before start |
| Order hardware (laptop, accessories) | IT | 5 days before start |
| Create email account and system credentials | IT | 3 days before start |
| Assign a buddy/mentor from the team | Hiring Manager | 3 days before start |
| Book Day 1 orientation session | HR | 1 week before start |
| Prepare workspace (desk, badge, parking pass) | Facilities | 2 days before start |

---

## 4. Day 1 Onboarding Activities

All new employees must complete the following on their first day:

1. **Check-in with HR** — Collect ID badge, sign any remaining paperwork, confirm personal details in HRIS.
2. **IT Setup Session** — Receive laptop, configure accounts, set up multi-factor authentication (MFA), and complete mandatory security training (approximately 45 minutes).
3. **Company Orientation** — Attend the HR-led orientation covering company history, mission, values, policies, benefits, and code of conduct. Duration: 2 hours.
4. **Team Introduction** — Meet with hiring manager and team members. Receive a 30/60/90 day plan.
5. **Workspace Tour** — Facilities walk-through including emergency exits, kitchen, meeting rooms, and accessibility resources.

---

## 5. First 30 Days — Employee & Manager Responsibilities

### Employee
- Complete all assigned compliance training modules in the LMS within 7 days of start date.
- Schedule introductory meetings ("coffee chats") with at least 5 cross-functional colleagues.
- Review and acknowledge all HR policies in the HR Portal.
- Set up weekly 1:1 meetings with direct manager.

### Hiring Manager
- Conduct weekly check-ins for the first 30 days.
- Provide access to all relevant project materials, repositories, and internal wikis.
- Assign at least one meaningful deliverable in the first 2 weeks to accelerate ramp-up.
- Submit 30-day feedback to HR via the *Onboarding Review* form.

---

## 6. Required Onboarding Documentation

The following documents must be submitted to HR before or on Day 1:

- Government-issued photo ID (for I-9 / right-to-work verification)
- Signed employment contract
- Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
- Direct deposit bank details
- Emergency contact form
- Benefits enrollment form (must be completed within 30 days of start — elections cannot be changed outside open enrollment)

---

## 7. Approver Roles for Onboarding Requests

Submitted onboarding requests in the HR Portal are reviewed by the **HR Coordinator** assigned to the new hire's department. Escalations for delayed hardware, access provisioning issues, or missing documentation go to the **HR Business Partner** and the **IT Manager**.
