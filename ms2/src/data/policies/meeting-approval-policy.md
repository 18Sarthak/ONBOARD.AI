# Meeting Room & Travel Approval Policy

**Policy ID:** OPS-001  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Operations Department  
**Last Reviewed:** September 2025

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the guidelines and approval requirements for booking meeting rooms and authorizing business travel at Acme Corp. It ensures that company resources are allocated responsibly and that travel expenditures align with departmental budgets.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time employees, contractors, and interns who require internal meeting spaces or who undertake business travel on behalf of Acme Corp.

---

## 3. Meeting Room Booking

### 3.1 Standard Rooms (Capacity ≤ 10 people)

Employees may book standard conference rooms directly through the internal calendar system (Google Calendar or Outlook) without managerial approval. Bookings are limited to **4 hours per session** and **8 hours per week per employee**.

### 3.2 Large Rooms (Capacity > 10 people or Executive Suite)

Large conference rooms and the Executive Suite require **prior written approval from the employee's direct manager**. Requests must be submitted at least **48 hours in advance** via the HR Portal under *Operations > Room Requests*.

**Required fields for large room requests:**
- Employee name and department
- Meeting purpose and business justification
- Expected attendee count
- Preferred date and time (provide two alternatives)
- Duration of booking

### 3.3 External Vendors or Clients

Meetings involving external vendors or clients in company premises must be approved by both the **direct manager** and the **department head**. Security must be notified 24 hours in advance to arrange visitor badges.

---

## 4. Business Travel

### 4.1 Domestic Travel

All domestic travel (within the country) requires approval from the employee's **direct manager**. Requests must be submitted a minimum of **5 business days before the travel date** via the HR Portal under *Finance > Travel Requests*.

**Required fields for domestic travel:**
- Employee name, department, and employee ID
- Travel destination (city and specific site/office)
- Purpose of travel and business justification
- Proposed dates of departure and return
- Estimated budget breakdown (transport, accommodation, meals, incidentals)
- Whether any client meetings are scheduled

### 4.2 International Travel

International travel requires approval from both the **direct manager** and the **VP of Finance**. Requests must be submitted a minimum of **10 business days** in advance.

Additional requirements:
- Passport and visa validity confirmation
- Travel insurance certificate (provided by HR upon approval)
- Per-diem rates approved by Finance

### 4.3 Reimbursement

All travel expenses must be submitted via the **Expense Report** module within **15 business days** of returning. Receipts are required for any single expense over $25. Expenses submitted late may not be reimbursed.

---

## 5. Approver Roles

| Request Type                  | Primary Approver    | Secondary Approver |
|-------------------------------|---------------------|--------------------|
| Standard room booking         | Self-service        | N/A                |
| Large room booking            | Direct Manager      | N/A                |
| Vendor/client meeting         | Direct Manager      | Department Head    |
| Domestic travel               | Direct Manager      | N/A                |
| International travel          | Direct Manager      | VP of Finance      |

---

## 6. Policy Violations

Unapproved bookings or travel will not be reimbursed and may result in disciplinary action. Repeated violations will be escalated to HR.
