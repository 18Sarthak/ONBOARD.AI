# Equipment & Software Request Policy

**Policy ID:** IT-004  
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2024  
**Owner:** IT Department  
**Last Reviewed:** July 2025

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the process by which Acme Corp employees may request hardware equipment and software licenses necessary for their work. It establishes budget thresholds, approval tiers, and turnaround expectations to ensure efficient provisioning while maintaining fiscal control.

---

## 2. Scope

Applicable to all employees, contractors, and interns who require company-owned or company-licensed technology resources to perform their duties.

---

## 3. Standard Equipment Catalogue

Acme Corp maintains a standard equipment catalogue that all employees may request without additional justification. Items include:

- **Laptop:** MacBook Air (M-series) or Dell XPS 15 — standard issue for all employees
- **Peripherals:** USB-C hub, wired keyboard and mouse, laptop stand
- **Monitor:** 27-inch 4K display (one per employee; second monitor requires manager approval)
- **Headset:** Noise-cancelling USB headset for remote/hybrid roles
- **Webcam:** 1080p webcam (for employees without built-in camera)

Standard catalogue items are approved automatically upon submission. Fulfillment time is **3–5 business days** for in-stock items.

---

## 4. Non-Standard Hardware Requests

Hardware not in the standard catalogue (e.g., high-performance workstations, specialized peripherals, ergonomic chairs, standing desks) requires approval based on cost:

| Item Cost          | Required Approver(s)                        |
|--------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Under $300         | Direct Manager                              |
| $300 – $1,500      | Direct Manager + Department Head            |
| $1,500 – $5,000    | Direct Manager + Department Head + IT Manager |
| Over $5,000        | VP of Operations + CFO                      |

**Required fields for non-standard hardware requests:**
- Employee name, department, and employee ID
- Item description and link to product page or specification sheet
- Business justification: why the standard catalogue does not meet the need
- Estimated cost (with vendor quote if over $1,500)
- Urgency: standard (2 weeks) or expedited (3 days, subject to availability)

---

## 5. Software License Requests

### 5.1 Pre-Approved Software

The following software is pre-approved and can be self-installed from the company software portal (Okta SSO):

- Google Workspace (Docs, Sheets, Meet, Drive)
- Slack, Zoom, Notion
- GitHub (standard repo access)
- Figma, Linear, Jira (role-dependent)
- VS Code, JetBrains IDEs (developer roles)

### 5.2 Software Not in Pre-Approved List

All other software — including AI tools, specialized analytics platforms, and third-party SaaS subscriptions — requires a formal request:

| Annual Cost        | Required Approver(s)                          |
|--------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Under $500/year    | Direct Manager                                |
| $500 – $5,000/year | Direct Manager + IT Manager                   |
| Over $5,000/year   | Direct Manager + IT Manager + VP of Finance   |

**Security review required** for any software that accesses or processes company data, customer data, or integrates with internal systems. IT Security will respond within **5 business days**.

**Required fields for software requests:**
- Employee name, department, and employee ID
- Software name, vendor, and official website
- Business use case and which team/project will use it
- Proposed subscription tier and annual cost
- Data sensitivity: does the tool access sensitive company or customer data?

---

## 6. Approval Turnaround Times

| Request Type              | Turnaround (after all docs received) |
|---------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Standard catalogue item   | Automatic (3–5 days fulfillment)     |
| Non-standard hardware < $300 | 2 business days                   |
| Non-standard hardware $300+ | 5 business days                    |
| Pre-approved software     | Immediate (self-service)             |
| Non-standard software     | 7 business days (+ security review) |

---

## 7. Equipment Return

All company-owned hardware must be returned upon resignation, termination, or role change. Employees have **5 business days** after their last day to return equipment. IT will provide a prepaid shipping label for remote employees. Unreturned equipment will be deducted from the final paycheck at replacement cost.

---

## 8. Contact

Submit all requests through the HR Portal under *IT Services > Equipment & Software*. For urgent needs, contact **itsupport@acmecorp.com** or Slack `#it-helpdesk`.
